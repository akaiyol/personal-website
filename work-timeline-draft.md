## Animation options — proposals, not approved

### 1. Career circuit

The timeline behaves like a thin electrical trace. Dragging a rectangular marker along it sends a short pulse into the nearest role. A small character-art symbol briefly operates: a truck follows a route at Visfuture, database rows connect at SAP, and agent nodes exchange a signal at RBC. One mechanism ties the page together while each role has its own behaviour.

### 2. Continuous transforming drawing

A small line/Braille drawing beside the timeline changes as the visitor moves between roles: a building plan becomes a routing map, then a database, then an agent network. Existing strokes rearrange into the next subject rather than replacing the artwork with a fade. This expresses the progression from electrical engineering into software and product.

### 3. Career tape reel

Dates sit along a narrow vertical punched tape. Dragging the tape moves it through a fixed reading bracket, selecting the corresponding experience. Text remains stationary and readable while the tape advances and settles into place.
