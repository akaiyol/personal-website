# Planet material source

`jupiter-voyager.jpg`: https://space.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/pix/jup0vss1.jpg . Published in JPL’s planetary map catalogue: https://space.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/jupiter.html . Data: Voyager; creator: David Seal; owner: JPL/Caltech; 720×360. Retrieved 2026-11-13.

The catalogue describes this as an older, imperfectly processed representative map. It is used here as a bounded source-quality comparison, not a scientific reconstruction. The artwork recolours the map and adds fictional large rings; it is not a natural-colour view of Jupiter or Saturn. Original source bytes are retained with attribution. A newer Hubble map was identified but its download endpoint timed out.
